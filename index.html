About GraciEvents

We turn meaningful moments into memorable experiences.

GraciEvents is a Ugandan events company dedicated to creating beautiful, well-organized and stress-free celebrations. From the first planning conversation to the final guest departure, we bring creativity, coordination and attention to detail to every occasion.

Under the leadership of Madam Grace Nsamba, our team works closely with families, couples, organizations and communities to design events that reflect their style, culture, budget and purpose.
